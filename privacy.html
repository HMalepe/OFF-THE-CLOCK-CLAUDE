The responsible party is [legal name of the person or company that runs Off The Clock], [physical address]. Our Information Officer is [name], reachable at [email address].

2. What we collect

  • Newsletter: your email address, when you subscribe.
  • Enquiry form: your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message.
  • Clock-out check: nothing. Your answers stay in your browser and are never stored or sent to us.

  • To send you the newsletter you asked for (based on your consent — you can withdraw it any time with the unsubscribe link).

  • To keep the website secure and working.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for anything you wouldn’t reasonably expect.

Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Enquiries are kept for [e.g. 12 months] after we last correspond, unless we need them longer for a booking or agreement.

You may ask us to confirm what information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to stop using it. You can also object to direct marketing at any time. Contact our Information Officer at [email address].
